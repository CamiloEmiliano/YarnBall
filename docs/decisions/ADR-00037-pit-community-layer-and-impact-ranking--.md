# ADR-00037: Point-in-Time Community Layer, Impact-Ranked Context & Report Coverage

- **ADR ID**: ADR-00037
- **Filename**: `ADR-00037-pit-community-layer-and-impact-ranking--.md`
- **Date**: 2026-11-22 08:48 UTC
- **Status**: PROPOSED
- **Target Subsystem**: `knowledge_graph` / `retrieval` / `explorer` / `tools`
- **Linked Commits**: Pending acceptance

---

## 1. Context & Problem Statement

1. **No global retrieval layer.** Neither Memgraph nor Postgres stores graph communities or community summaries for financial entities. (`graphify-out/` communities are code-navigation artifacts only.) Thematic or portfolio-wide questions, such as *"Which S&P 500 clusters depend on Taiwanese foundry capacity as of 2022-Q3?"*, can only be approximated by many ego traversals.
2. **Hop-only context ranking.** `HopOrderedSubgraphVerbalizer` ([`retrieval/pipeline.py`](../../retrieval/pipeline.py)) sorts edges by hop ascending and clamps to `MAX_SUBGRAPH_EDGES = 25`. With BFS defaults of hop 5 (cap 10), the frontier explodes, and truncation keeps low-impact 1-hop edges while dropping high-impact deep edges that `SparseNetworkCascadeEngine` just quantified.
3. **Coverage is invisible in the report.** The verbalizer adds a free-text note (*"N additional distant edges pruned..."*) to the LLM prompt only. `OperationalContagionReport` ([`retrieval/reporting.py`](../../retrieval/reporting.py)) has no structured fields for truncation, degraded data ([`ADR-00036`](ADR-00036-fail-fast-infra-and-deployable-topology-.md)), excluded unanchored edges ([`ADR-00033`](ADR-00033-bitemporal-pit-contract-for-read-paths--.md)), legacy evidence ([`ADR-00035`](ADR-00035-text-unit-chunk-layer-and-evidence-links.md)), or how much of the propagated impact rests on default cascade parameters ([`ADR-00026`](ADR-00026-cascade-parameter-extraction-provenance-.md)). "No exposure", "no data", and "assumption-driven" look the same to Domain 2.

---

## 2. Decision Drivers & Constraints

1. **Point-in-time safety (why communities uniquely require materialized checkpoints)**: unlike graph edges and `node_embeddings`—which use `[valid_from, valid_to)` intervals without periodic snapshots ([`ADR-00033`](ADR-00033-bitemporal-pit-contract-for-read-paths--.md))—community clusters and LLM summaries aggregate whole-graph topology into prose and cannot be `WHERE`-filtered retroactively. They must be computed over the structural topology as of `as_of` and cached by `(universe_id, as_of)`.
2. **Coverage-universe scoping**: communities are computed over the structural investable universe + supply-chain closure (`kg_edges` / `G_resolved`, excluding transient `:News` impulses per [`ADR-00036`](ADR-00036-fail-fast-infra-and-deployable-topology-.md)), never scoped to a single Domain 2 trading portfolio's current holdings.
3. **Presentability**: a report has to state its own coverage and assumptions in a form that both machines and the Workbench can read.
4. **Licensing and dependencies**: avoid copyleft graph libraries (`leidenalg` / `python-igraph` are GPL). `networkx` (BSD, 3.6.1 currently installed as a transitive dependency) offers Louvain, and the `memgraph-mage` image already in [`docker-compose.yml`](../../docker-compose.yml) ships community detection.
5. **Local model budget**: summaries are generated by `yarnball-qwen:7b` through `YarnBallModelClient`.

---

## 3. Considered Alternatives & Rejection Rationale

- **Alternative A: Hierarchical Leiden via `leidenalg`.** Rejected because of the GPL license (see drivers).
- **Alternative B: Communities on live Memgraph through MAGE only.** Rejected as the primary path. It isn't point-in-time and not reproducible offline. It can serve as the `live` mode backend.
- **Alternative C: Raise `MAX_SUBGRAPH_EDGES`.** Rejected. That reintroduces the clutter cliff the 25-edge budget exists to prevent ([`ADR-00006`](ADR-00006-phase-7-sparse-network-cascade-engine---.md)).

---

## 4. Decision Outcome

1. **Point-in-time structural communities, new `knowledge_graph/communities.py`**:
   - `build_communities(as_of, *, universe_id="sp500_pit", levels=2) -> list[Community]` runs deterministic seeded `networkx` Louvain (weight = edge `share`, default 1.0) over structural edges active at `as_of` from the resolved Postgres ledger / Parquet export (`G_resolved`), recursively for a two-level hierarchy.
   - `networkx` becomes an explicit pinned requirement.
   - Built lazily on demand (and cached) for any requested `(universe_id, as_of)` or materialized at quarterly checkpoints by [`tools/build_kg_snapshot.py`](../../tools/build_kg_snapshot.py).
2. **Community reports**: Postgres `community_reports(universe_id, as_of, level, community_id, member_ids TEXT[], summary TEXT, key_edges JSONB, evidence_chunk_ids TEXT[], embedding vector(EMBEDDING_DIM), PRIMARY KEY (universe_id, as_of, level, community_id))`, plus optional `communities.parquet` in offline snapshot bundles. Summaries come from `YarnBallModelClient` (new task `COMMUNITY_REPORT`), resolving passage snippets from Postgres `document_chunks` via `evidence_chunk_ids` ([`ADR-00035`](ADR-00035-text-unit-chunk-layer-and-evidence-links.md)).
3. **Query routing in `query_bi`** ([`ADR-00034`](ADR-00034-single-bi-read-facade-and-pipeline-merge.md)):
   - Rules first: a resolved entity mention routes to `local`. Thematic, sector, or broad risk-concentration phrasing with no anchor entity routes to `global`.
   - `global` runs map-reduce over `community_reports` restricted to the latest `as_of <= ctx.as_of` for the active `universe_id`.
4. **Impact-ranked verbalization**: rank by `(cascade_impact desc, hop asc, provenance_rank desc)`, where `cascade_impact` is the edge's contribution to propagated loss from `SparseNetworkCascadeEngine` and `provenance_rank` orders extracted > bounded > default. The 25-edge budget is kept.
5. **Report coverage block**: `OperationalContagionReport.coverage: ReportCoverage`:
   ```
   data_status: Literal["ok", "degraded", "no_coverage"]
   edges_total: int; edges_shown: int; truncated: bool; dropped_impact_share: float
   unanchored_excluded: int; legacy_unchunked_evidence: int
   default_param_impact_share: float   # share of propagated impact resting on default share/buffer_days
   ```
   [`explorer/static/app.js`](../../explorer/static/app.js) shows persistent badges: *Truncated*, *Assumption-driven* (when `default_param_impact_share >= 0.5`), *Degraded*, *No coverage*.

---

## 5. Invariants & Guardrails

1. **Point-in-time communities**: no `community_reports` row for `as_of = T` references an edge with `valid_from > T`.
2. **Coverage always present**: every `OperationalContagionReport` carries a populated `coverage` block, and `data_status = "ok"` is impossible while any store is degraded.
3. **Truncation is never silent**: `truncated = true` whenever `edges_total > edges_shown`.
4. **Domain isolation preserved**: `coverage` holds operational metadata only, with no trading or portfolio fields ([`ADR-00011`](ADR-00011-operational-contagion-report-schema-----.md)).

---

## 6. Consequences & Downstream Impact

- **Positive / Guarantees**:
  - Thematic and portfolio-level BI questions become answerable without leakage.
  - The context budget keeps the edges that matter.
  - Domain 2 and analysts can tell "no exposure" from "no data" from "assumption-driven".
- **Negative / Trade-offs**:
  - Snapshot builds gain a community detection pass plus an LLM summarization pass.
  - Louvain is non-deterministic unless seeded, so seeds must be fixed for reproducibility.
  - The report schema change must be propagated to the ADR-00013 time-series join.

## 7. Open Questions for Review

1. **Algorithm**: `networkx` Louvain (BSD, offline) or MAGE community detection (live only)? Is two hierarchy levels enough?
2. **Assumption-driven threshold**: is `default_param_impact_share >= 0.5` the right badge threshold?
3. **Routing**: rules-only routing first, or a `YarnBallModelClient` classification task from the start?
